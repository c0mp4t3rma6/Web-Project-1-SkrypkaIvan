My Education

Mykolaiv Municipal Collegium
I have studied in this school for almost 4 years almost finishing 4 grades with the same teacher, but for war reasons I had to move to a different country. After moving a country I have still studied there online until the end of my grade 7 year.
Tallinn School No.21
The richest school I have ever studied in and even though only up until end of grade 4 that was a very nice and unusual experience.
Räägu School
That was my first and only school for Ukrainian people out of Ukraine. I have studied there again through grade 4 because kids in Estonia go to school a year later and until almost the middle of my grade 5 year. It was a fun experience of uniting with people that are supposed to be similar to me but still are from different regions of Ukraine. I loved it there but they were not the best teachers.
Sakala Eragümnaasium
Probably the smallest and at the same time the most fancy school I have ever been to. Great teachers, experience, and people all are accommodated by a small almost family like community which only has one class per parallel. That was the most unusual experience in my life and for almost 8 months of studying it was worth it!
École South Pointe School
Great school with some of the best teachers I have met which made me do my best in a new community with not the highest level of language, but I still did my best and got all my marks on the greatest level.
Pembina Trails Collegiate
Since the start of grade 9 I have been here and even though it was an unusual start I am fully integrated now and will probably have a lot to say after more time spent here!
